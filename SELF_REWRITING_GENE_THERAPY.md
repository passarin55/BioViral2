# Self-Rewriting Gene Therapy: Adaptive Multi-Round Editing

## Overview

**Revolutionary Concept:** Gene editing systems that automatically adapt to viral evolution, deploying successive editing rounds triggered by immune responses and viral escape detection.

**Key Innovation:** Instead of one-time genetic modification, the system becomes **self-updating** through programmed feedback loops that sense viral evolution and activate pre-positioned secondary edits.

---

## The Self-Rewriting Model

### Why Self-Rewriting is Needed

**Problem:** Viruses evolve resistance mechanisms
```
Year 0-2: CCR5 deletion blocks HIV entry → Viral control
Year 2-3: Virus evolves X4-tropic (CXCL12-using) → Escape
Year 3-5: Virus upregulates Vif → APOBEC3G antagonism → Re-escape
```

**Traditional Solution:** Multiple sequential treatments
- Patient undergoes Gene Therapy Round 1
- Wait 2 years for escape
- Return for Gene Therapy Round 2
- Repeat every 2-3 years

**Self-Rewriting Solution:** Pre-programmed automatic responses
```
Year 0: All 4 editing rounds pre-delivered (inactivated)
        ├─ Round 1: Immediately active (CCR5 + CXCL12)
        ├─ Round 2: Dormant, waiting for trigger
        ├─ Round 3: Dormant, deeper contingency
        └─ Round 4: Dormant, ultimate defense

Year 2-3: Viral escape detected by immune system
        → Round 2 automatically activates (TRIM5α + APOBEC3G)
        → No clinic visit needed

Year 4-5: Further evolution detected
        → Round 3 automatically activates (Prime editing triple)
        → System regenerates defenses continuously

Year 6+: Permanent antiviral state achieved
        → Round 4 epigenetic lock-in
        → Functional cure
```

**Problem:** Immune responses to repeated AAV vectors

**Solution: Serotype Diversity**
```
Round 1: AAV6 (hepatotropic) - Initial delivery

```
