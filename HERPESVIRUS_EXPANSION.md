### 3. Human Herpesvirus 8 (HHV-8) - KSHV
**Genus:** Rhadinovirus | **Transmission:** Contact

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Threat Level** | **MEDIUM** ⚠️ |
| **Genome Size** | 165,263 bp |

| **Druggable Sites** | K1-signaling, Glycoprotein complex, Viral kinase, DNA-polymerase |
| **PDB Structure** | 5V8P |
| **Vaccine** | No |

**Clinical Significance:**
- Kaposi Sarcoma-associated Herpesvirus (KSHV)
- Endemic in Mediterranean, African, and Middle Eastern regions
- Causes Kaposi sarcoma, multicentric Castleman disease, and primary effusion lymphoma
- Major concern in AIDS patients (pre-HAART era)
- Viral G-protein coupled receptors (vGPCRs) unique among herpesviruses

## Key Enhancements

### EBV (EBNA & LMP Proteins)
- **EBNA1**: Binds viral episomal DNA, essential for replication
- **EBNA2**: Drives B-cell proliferation, key transformation protein
- **LMP1**: Mimics CD40 signaling, constitutively activates NF-κB
- **LMP2**: Suppresses B-cell receptor signaling, maintains latency

These oncoproteins are critical for understanding EBV-driven lymphomas.

### HHV-8 (K1 Protein)
- Unique viral GPCR analog
- Constitutively active signaling
- Drives MAPK and PI3K pathways
- Novel drug target for KSHV-associated malignancies
